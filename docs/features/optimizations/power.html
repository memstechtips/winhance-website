Power Management & Plans

Your power plan and the advanced power options behind it: display and sleep timeouts, processor power, USB, PCI Express and battery.

Flip any switch in the window above to see what Winhance would write to Windows.

The active power plan and the settings inside it: when the display and PC sleep, what the power button and lid do, and how the processor, USB, PCI Express and graphics save power. Winhance shows a power setting only when this PC's power plan has it, so your list may be shorter than this page.

Advanced processor settings

Some processor settings are hidden in Windows' own Power Options. Winhance keeps them locked until you click one and confirm a warning, because a wrong value can cause instability, slowdowns or heat problems. Tick Don't show this warning again for advanced power settings to unlock them all.

Technical Details

Each setting below lists exactly what Winhance reads and writes: registry values, scheduled tasks, power settings and scripts, with the recommended value and the Windows default marked. It is the same table the app shows when you turn on Technical Details in the View menu.
